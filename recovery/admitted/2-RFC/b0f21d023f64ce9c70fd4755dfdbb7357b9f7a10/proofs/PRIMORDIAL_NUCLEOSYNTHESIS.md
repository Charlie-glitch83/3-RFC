## Lemma E4 — Reaction-graph legality

The abundance of isotope `a` is the expectation of its occupation count divided by the conserved baryon count. Applying the configuration generator gives the stoichiometric route sum exactly. Thus the abundance ODE is a moment identity of the exact nuclear process rather than an independently assumed network.
