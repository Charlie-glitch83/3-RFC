# The Relationship of CIF, QV, and RFL

The triad can be misunderstood in two opposite ways.

One mistake is to separate CIF, QV, and RFL too much, as if they were independent substances.

The opposite mistake is to collapse them too much, as if they were interchangeable names for the same thing.

The source files support neither extreme.

The correct view is:

```text
one emergence event
three ordered roles
```

## One event

CIF, QV, and RFL are not three unrelated objects. They are phases of one act of becoming.

The act is:

```text
possibility becoming inheritable memory through witnessed crossing
```

CIF is that act seen from the source side.

QV is that act seen at the crossing.

RFL is that act seen as stabilized memory.

## Three roles

Although they are one event, the roles cannot be swapped.

CIF is not QV. Potential is not the crossing.

QV is not RFL. Crossing is not memory.

RFL is not CIF. Memory is not source.

The roles are bound together but phase-distinct.

## The component relations

### CIF to QV

CIF gives QV something to act on.

QV gives CIF a way to become actual.

Without QV, CIF remains open. Without CIF, QV is empty.

### QV to RFL

QV produces the crossing. RFL is what stabilizes after crossing.

Without RFL, QV is event without history. Without QV, RFL is unexplained structure.

### RFL to CIF

RFL returns as memory that conditions the next CIF.

But RFL does not become the source-parent.

The bridge is `M_rec`, the inheritance membrane.

## The breathing rhythm

The triad has a breathing rhythm:

```text
open -> narrow -> stabilize -> reopen conditioned by memory
```

Or:

```text
CIF opens
QV narrows
RFL stabilizes
M_rec returns
CIF opens again, now conditioned
```

This breathing rhythm explains how the triad can be infinitely simple and infinitely generative.

## The danger of overidentification

If one says, "CIF, QV, and RFL are all the same thing," precision is lost.

The better statement is:

```text
They are the same emergence event viewed from three non-interchangeable phases.
```

This keeps unity without destroying orientation.

## The danger of separation

If one says, "CIF, QV, and RFL are separate substances," the act is lost.

The triad is not a machine assembled from parts. It is a movement differentiated into roles.

## Final relation

```text
CIF is the openness of the act.
QV is the crossing of the act.
RFL is the memory of the act.
```

The triad is the act itself.
